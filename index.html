New Project: "Layers: RAREAWHERE"

What even is RAREAWHERE?
Layers: “Rareawhere” follows the story of a new student named Cambi in Rareawhere High School, though the school can’t decide which classes he should attend, so he switches every day!
And how is RAREAWHERE related?
RAREAWHERE takes place just as SURREALITY (chapter 1) begins. This short game focuses on Cambi's perspective of the chapter 1 events, and how he interacts with Dave, Masie, and... Kaisei?

LAYERS of what?

Layers is a 4-Chapter game about discovering and adventuring a whole new world, the Layers world. You are Dave, a young kid who loves making friends but hates talking. Your friends are Sevil and Masie. Play "SURREALITY" (CH1) to learn about them.
